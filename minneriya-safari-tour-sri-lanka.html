About Minneriya National Park

Why Travelers Visit Minneriya

Minneriya National Park surrounds the ancient Minneriya Tank, built in the 3rd century, and is famous worldwide for hosting one of nature's greatest wildlife spectacles.

The Elephant Gathering

Between July and October, hundreds of wild elephants gather along the receding tank shoreline to drink, bathe and graze on fresh grass, forming the largest known assembly of Asian elephants in the world.

Nature & Photography

Capture golden evening light over the lake, playful calves and majestic herds under the guidance of a driver who knows the park's rhythms well.

Rich Biodiversity

Beyond elephants, spot spotted deer, sambar, water buffalo, crocodiles and over 170 recorded bird species around the wetlands.

Tour Information

Duration

Safaris typically run 3-4 hours, with morning and late-afternoon departures available depending on your schedule.

Pickup Options

Hotel pickup from Habarana, Sigiriya, Polonnaruwa and nearby areas. Longer-distance transfers can also be arranged.

Best Time to Visit

July to October is ideal, with the Great Elephant Gathering peaking in August and September when the tank water is lowest.

Vehicle & Booking

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time for the Elephant Gathering?

The gathering builds from around July and peaks in August and September, when the largest herds appear around the tank.

How long is a Minneriya Safari tour?

Most safaris run for around 3-4 hours, timed for morning coolness or late-afternoon golden light.

Can LetsGo arrange hotel pickup?
