Tour Introduction

About the Destination

The Temple of the Tooth, or Sri Dalada Maligawa, sits beside the scenic Kandy Lake and is regarded as one of the most sacred Buddhist sites in the world.

Why Visit

A must-see for anyone interested in Sri Lanka's spiritual heritage, royal history and living Buddhist traditions.

Tour Highlights

Witness the daily ritual offerings, admire intricate carvings and murals, and learn the story of the sacred tooth relic.

Nearby Attractions

Combine your visit with a walk around Kandy Lake, the Royal Botanical Gardens in Peradeniya or a Kandyan cultural dance show.

Tour Information

Best Time to Visit

Early morning or evening ritual times offer the most meaningful experience, with the annual Esala Perahera festival a major highlight.

Tour Duration

A half-day visit is common, and it pairs well with other Kandy attractions for a full day tour.

Dress Code

Modest dress covering shoulders and knees is required, and footwear must be removed before entering the shrine.

Booking Details

Advance booking helps us tailor the trip and provide a smoother visit.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Temple of the Tooth?

It is a sacred Buddhist temple in Kandy that houses the relic of the tooth of the Buddha, making it one of the most important pilgrimage sites in the world.

Is there a dress code?

Yes, visitors must dress modestly with shoulders and knees covered, and shoes must be removed before entering.

Can I combine this with other tours?

Yes, it pairs well with Kandy Lake, the Royal Botanical Gardens or a traditional Kandyan dance performance.

Is transport included?

Absolutely. LetsGo offers transport to and from Kandy and nearby attractions.
