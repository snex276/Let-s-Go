Tour Introduction

About Matara City

Matara is a charming city in Southern Sri Lanka known for its history, coastal beauty, temples, and lively local culture. It is a favorite destination for travelers seeking a mix of heritage and seaside charm.

Why Visit

Tourists visit Matara for its historical fortifications, sacred religious sites, warm hospitality, delicious food, and easy access to scenic beaches along the southern coast.

Top Places to Visit

Explore Matara Fort, Star Fort, Parevi Duwa Temple, Weherahena Temple, Polhena Beach, and the Dondra Lighthouse on a flexible and comfortable city tour.

Activities & Experiences

Enjoy historical sightseeing, coastal views, local food stops, photography moments, and a calm exploration of Matara’s most beloved places.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best places to visit in Matara?

Matara Fort, Star Fort, Parevi Duwa Temple, Weherahena Temple, Polhena Beach, and Dondra Lighthouse are among the top attractions.

How long does a Matara city tour take?

A typical Matara sightseeing tour lasts between 4 to 8 hours depending on the route and your interests.

Can LetsGo arrange hotel pickup?

Yes, pickup and drop-off can be arranged from your hotel or accommodation in Matara and nearby areas.

Is Matara suitable for photography?

Absolutely. Matara offers coastal landscapes, old architecture, temples, and vibrant street scenes for memorable photos.
