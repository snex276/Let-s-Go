Tour Introduction

About the Destination

Arugam Bay is Sri Lanka's premier east coast surf destination, known for its crescent bay and world-renowned point break.

Why Visit

Perfect for surfers and travelers seeking a laid-back coastal escape away from the busier west and south coasts.

Tour Highlights

Enjoy sunrise surf sessions, scenic views from Elephant Rock and easy access to Kumana National Park's wildlife.

Activities & Experiences

Try surfing, beach lagoon visits, a Kumana safari or simply unwind at a beachfront cafe with an ocean view.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Arugam Bay good for surfing?

Yes, it is home to one of the world's best right-hand point breaks and suits both beginners and experienced surfers.

What is the best season for Arugam Bay?

The east coast season runs from May to September, opposite to the west and south coast seasons.

Can I combine this with other tours?

Yes, it pairs well with a Kumana National Park safari or other east coast attractions.

Is transport included?

Absolutely. LetsGo offers transport to and from Arugam Bay and nearby attractions.
