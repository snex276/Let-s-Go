Tour Introduction

About the Knuckles Mountain Range

The Knuckles Mountain Range is part of Sri Lanka's Central Highlands UNESCO World Heritage Site, named for its series of peaks resembling a clenched fist. It offers dramatic scenery, cloud forest, and traditional village life largely untouched by mass tourism.

Why Visit

Travelers visit the Knuckles Range for its remote hiking trails, panoramic viewpoints, rich biodiversity, and a chance to experience rural highland village culture.

Top Places to Visit

Visit Corbett's Gap viewpoint, the Pitawala Pathana grasslands, Nitro Caves, Riverston, and traditional villages such as Meemure nestled deep within the range.

Activities & Experiences

Experience guided mountain hiking, scenic viewpoint stops, village trekking, birdwatching, and camping under clear highland skies.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best places to visit in the Knuckles Range?

Corbett's Gap, the Pitawala Pathana grasslands, Nitro Caves, and Riverston are popular highlights of the range.

Do I need hiking experience for the Knuckles Range?

Trails vary in difficulty, from easy viewpoint walks to more demanding multi-day treks, so options exist for most fitness levels.

How can I book a Knuckles Mountain Range Tour?

You can book directly through WhatsApp, the mobile app, or by contacting LetsGo through the website.

Can I visit a traditional village in the range?

Absolutely. Many travelers include a trek to Meemure, a remote village nestled within the Knuckles Range, as part of their tour.
