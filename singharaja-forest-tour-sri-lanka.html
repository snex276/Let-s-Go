Tour Introduction

About Sinharaja Forest

Sinharaja Forest Reserve is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and Sri Lanka's last significant tract of primary lowland rainforest. It shelters an extraordinary concentration of endemic plants, birds, and wildlife found nowhere else in the world.

Why Visit

Travelers visit Sinharaja for its untouched biodiversity, guided rainforest hikes, rare bird sightings, and a deep immersion into Sri Lanka's natural heritage.

Top Places to Visit

Explore the Kudawa entrance trails, the Sinha Raja Falls, Mulawella viewpoint, and the reserve's dense canopy walking paths with a licensed forest guide.

Activities & Experiences

Experience guided rainforest hiking, birdwatching for endemic species, waterfall visits, and learning about the reserve's unique plant and insect life.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best places to visit in Sinharaja?

The Kudawa entrance trails, Sinha Raja Falls, and Mulawella viewpoint are popular highlights inside the reserve.

Do I need a guide to enter Sinharaja?

Yes, a licensed forest guide is required to enter the reserve, and LetsGo can help arrange this as part of your tour.

How can I book a Sinharaja Forest Tour?

You can book directly through WhatsApp, the mobile app, or by contacting LetsGo through the website.

What should I wear for the hike?

Light, quick-drying clothing, sturdy walking shoes, and rain protection are recommended, as the trail can be muddy and humid.
