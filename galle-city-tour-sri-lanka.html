Tour Introduction

About Galle City

Galle is one of Sri Lanka’s most captivating coastal cities, famous for its UNESCO-listed fort, colonial heritage, and relaxed atmosphere. It is a leading destination for visitors seeking history, beaches, and scenic beauty.

Why Visit

Travelers visit Galle for the iconic fort, ocean views, cultural landmarks, local markets, and nearby beaches that make the city feel timeless and vibrant.

Top Places to Visit

Visit Galle Fort, the Galle Lighthouse, Dutch Reformed Church, National Maritime Museum, Unawatuna Beach, Jungle Beach, and the Japanese Peace Pagoda.

Activities & Experiences

Experience colonial architecture, seaside walks, cultural stops, beach visits, market exploring, and great photography opportunities throughout the city.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best places to visit in Galle?

Galle Fort, the lighthouse, Dutch Reformed Church, the maritime museum, Unawatuna Beach, Jungle Beach, and the Japanese Peace Pagoda are popular highlights.

Is Galle Fort worth visiting?

Yes, Galle Fort is one of the most iconic heritage sites in Sri Lanka and offers scenic views, history, and photogenic streets.

How can I book a Galle City Tour?

You can book directly through WhatsApp, the mobile app, or by contacting LetsGo through the website.

Can I include beaches in the tour?

Absolutely. Many travelers pair the city tour with beach stops such as Unawatuna or Jungle Beach.
