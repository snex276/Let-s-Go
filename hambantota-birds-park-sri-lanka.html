Tour Introduction

About the Destination

Hambantota Birds Park is a wetland sanctuary in southern Sri Lanka, known for its rich biodiversity and abundant birdlife around its lagoons and marshes.

Why Visit

A rewarding stop for birdwatchers, photographers and nature lovers seeking a calm, scenic escape close to Hambantota town.

Tour Highlights

Observe colorful resident species and seasonal migratory birds up close from walking trails and viewing points around the wetlands.

Activities & Experiences

Tour Information

Best Time to Visit

Early morning and late afternoon offer the best birdwatching, with migratory species most visible from November to March.

Tour Duration

A half-day visit is common, and it pairs well with other Hambantota area attractions.

What to Bring

Binoculars, a camera and comfortable walking shoes are recommended for the best experience.

Booking Details

Frequently Asked Questions

What birds can I see at Hambantota Birds Park?

Visitors can spot herons, storks, pelicans, kingfishers and a variety of seasonal migratory species around the wetlands.

What is the best time for birdwatching?

Early morning and late afternoon are ideal, with the migratory season from November to March offering the widest variety.

Can I combine this with other tours?

Yes, it pairs well with a Yala or Bundala safari, or other nearby nature and wildlife attractions.

Is transport included?

Absolutely. LetsGo offers transport to and from Hambantota Birds Park and nearby attractions.
