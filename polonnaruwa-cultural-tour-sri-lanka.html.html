Tour Introduction

About the Destination

Polonnaruwa was Sri Lanka's medieval capital, and today its ruins remain among the best-preserved ancient cities in South Asia.

Why Visit

A rewarding stop for history lovers, offering a compact, well laid-out site that's easy to explore by bicycle or on foot.

Tour Highlights

See the Royal Palace ruins, the Vatadage circular relic house, the Gal Vihara rock statues and the vast Parakrama Samudra reservoir.

Nearby Attractions

Combine your visit with Sigiriya Rock Fortress or the Dambulla Cave Temple within the Cultural Triangle.

Tour Information

Best Time to Visit

Early morning offers cooler temperatures and better light for exploring the spread-out ruins.

Tour Duration

Getting Around

Frequently Asked Questions

What can I see at Polonnaruwa?

You can explore royal palace ruins, ancient temples, the Gal Vihara rock statues and a massive man-made reservoir.

Is Polonnaruwa easy to explore?

Yes, the ruins are well organized and relatively flat, making it easy to explore on foot or by bicycle.

Can I combine this with other tours?

Yes, it pairs very well with Sigiriya Rock Fortress and Dambulla Cave Temple on the same Cultural Triangle itinerary.

Is transport included?

Absolutely. LetsGo offers transport to and from Polonnaruwa and nearby attractions.
