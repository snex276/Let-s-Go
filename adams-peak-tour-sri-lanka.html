Tour Introduction

About Adam's Peak

Adam's Peak, also known as Sri Pada, is a 2,243-meter sacred mountain in the central highlands, home to a rock formation revered as a sacred footprint by four major faiths.

Why Visit

Travelers climb Adam's Peak for its spiritual significance, the challenge of the 5,500-step ascent, and the reward of a breathtaking sunrise from the summit.

Top Places to Visit

Pass the Japanese peace pagoda partway up the trail, ring the summit bell on arrival, and take in panoramic views over the tea-covered highlands.

Activities & Experiences

Experience an overnight pilgrimage-style hike, sunrise viewing, the mountain's shadow phenomenon, and tea stops with fellow climbers along the illuminated trail.

Tour Information

Duration

The ascent typically takes 3-5 hours depending on fitness and crowd levels, with a faster 1.5-2 hour descent.

Pickup Locations

We arrange pickup from Hatton, Nallathanniya, Dalhousie, Maskeliya and nearby hotels with flexible overnight timing.

Best Time to Visit

The pilgrimage season runs from December to May, when the trail is lit and fully serviced. Off-season climbs from June to November are quieter but less equipped.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time should I start climbing?

Most climbers start between 1:00 AM and 2:30 AM to reach the summit before the roughly 6:00 AM sunrise, earlier on weekends and full moon days.

How difficult is the Adam's Peak hike?

It involves around 5,500 steps and moderate fitness, but is achievable for most ages with steady pacing and rest stops along the way.

Is there an entrance fee?

No, the climb to Adam's Peak is free, though donations at the summit temple are welcomed.

How can I book an Adam's Peak Tour?
