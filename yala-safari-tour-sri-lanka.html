About Yala National Park

Why Travelers Visit Yala

Yala National Park is one of Sri Lanka's most famous wildlife destinations, known for its rich biodiversity, dramatic landscapes and extraordinary wildlife sightings.

Wildlife Experience

From leopards and elephants to sloth bears, crocodiles and a wide variety of birds, Yala offers one of the island's best safari adventures.

Nature & Photography

Capture stunning landscapes, golden light and close wildlife views under the guidance of a skilled driver who understands the park well.

Best for Adventure

Whether you are a wildlife lover, photographer or family traveler, Yala offers extraordinary moments in the wild.

Tour Information

Duration

Half-day or full-day safari options available depending on your schedule and travel preferences.

Pickup Options

Hotel pickup from Hambantota, Tissamaharama, Kirinda and nearby areas. Airport transfers can also be arranged.

Best Time to Visit

The best months are from February to July for better wildlife visibility and dry road conditions.

Vehicle & Booking

Private jeep safari with experienced driver-guide. Advance booking is recommended for the best availability.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time for Yala Safari?

The dry season from February to July is ideal for wildlife sightings and easier safari travel.

How long is a Yala Safari tour?

Most tours run for half a day or a full day, depending on your preferred pace and itinerary.

Can LetsGo arrange hotel pickup?

Yes. We can arrange pickup from your hotel, guesthouse or nearby town for a smooth safari start.

Is a private jeep safari available?

Absolutely. We offer private jeep safari options for couples, families and small groups.
