Tour Introduction

About Horton Plains

Horton Plains National Park is a highland plateau and UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its misty cloud forest, grasslands, and the dramatic escarpment of World's End. It's one of Sri Lanka's most rewarding nature destinations.

Why Visit

Travelers visit Horton Plains for its dramatic cliff views, cool misty trails, rare highland wildlife, and one of the best sunrise hiking experiences in Sri Lanka.

Top Places to Visit

Visit World's End, Mini World's End, Baker's Falls, the Horton Plains circular hiking trail, and the park's visitor and wildlife information center.

Activities & Experiences

Experience early morning hiking, cliff-edge viewpoints, waterfall visits, birdwatching, and spotting endemic wildlife along the plateau's grasslands.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best places to visit in Horton Plains?

World's End, Mini World's End, and Baker's Falls along the main circular trail are the top highlights.

Why is an early start recommended?

Clouds typically roll in by mid-morning, so arriving early gives the clearest views from World's End.

How can I book a Horton Plains Tour?

You can book directly through WhatsApp, the mobile app, or by contacting LetsGo through the website.

Is the hike suitable for beginners?

Yes, the main circular trail is a moderate walk suitable for most fitness levels, though warm clothing and good shoes are recommended.
