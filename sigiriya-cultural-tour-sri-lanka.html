Tour Introduction

About the Destination

Sigiriya, also known as Lion Rock, is an ancient rock fortress and palace ruin rising dramatically from the surrounding plains of central Sri Lanka.

Why Visit

A bucket-list destination for history lovers, photographers and travelers seeking one of Sri Lanka's most iconic landmarks.

Tour Highlights

See the famous Sigiriya frescoes, walk through the Mirror Wall gallery, pass beneath the Lion's Gate paws and reach the summit ruins.

Nearby Attractions

Combine your visit with Dambulla Cave Temple, Pidurangala Rock or the ancient city of Polonnaruwa within the Cultural Triangle.

Tour Information

Best Time to Visit

Early morning is ideal for a cooler climb, clearer skies and fewer crowds at the summit.

Tour Duration

A half-day visit is common, with the climb taking around 2 to 3 hours round trip.

What to Bring

Comfortable walking shoes, water and sun protection are recommended for the climb.

Booking Details

Advance booking helps us tailor the trip and provide a smoother visit.

Frequently Asked Questions

How difficult is the Sigiriya climb?

The climb involves stairs and some steep sections, but is manageable for most travelers with a moderate fitness level.

What will I see at the top?

The summit offers palace ruins, ancient pools and sweeping views over the surrounding jungle and plains.

Can I combine this with other tours?

Yes, it pairs well with Dambulla Cave Temple, Polonnaruwa or other Cultural Triangle attractions.

Is transport included?

Absolutely. LetsGo offers transport to and from Sigiriya and nearby attractions.
