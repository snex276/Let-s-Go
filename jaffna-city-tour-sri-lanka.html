Tour Introduction

About Jaffna City

Jaffna is the cultural capital of Sri Lanka's Tamil community, rich in unique cuisine, colorful temples, colonial history, and a distinct island charm shaped by its peninsula geography and lagoons.

Why Visit

Travelers visit Jaffna for its striking Dutch fort, colorful Hindu temples, unique regional cuisine, quiet beaches, and a culture distinct from the rest of Sri Lanka.

Top Places to Visit

Visit Jaffna Fort, Nallur Kandaswamy Temple, the Jaffna Public Library, Casuarina Beach, Jaffna Market, and the Nagadeepa Purana Vihara on Nainativu Island.

Activities & Experiences

Experience fort exploration, temple visits, library heritage tours, beach relaxation, local market browsing, and tasting authentic Jaffna cuisine.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best places to visit in Jaffna?

Jaffna Fort, Nallur Kandaswamy Temple, the Jaffna Public Library, Casuarina Beach, and Jaffna Market are popular highlights.

Is Jaffna Fort worth visiting?

Yes, it is one of the best-preserved Dutch forts in Asia and offers a fascinating look into the region's colonial history.

How can I book a Jaffna City Tour?

You can book directly through WhatsApp, the mobile app, or by contacting LetsGo through the website.

Can I include island visits in the tour?

Absolutely. Many travelers pair the city tour with a boat trip to Nainativu Island to visit the Nagadeepa temple.
