Tour Introduction

About Diyaluma Falls

Diyaluma Falls is one of Sri Lanka's tallest waterfalls, cascading down a sheer rock face near Koslanda in the island's hill country. It's best known for the natural infinity pools found at the top of the falls, a rewarding hike away.

Why Visit

Travelers visit Diyaluma Falls for its dramatic height, refreshing natural pools, scenic hiking trail, and sweeping views over the surrounding valley.

Top Places to Visit

Visit the base viewpoint of Diyaluma Falls, hike up to the natural infinity pools, and enjoy the panoramic views over the tea-covered valley below.

Activities & Experiences

Experience waterfall viewing, a scenic uphill hike, swimming in the natural rock pools, and photography of one of Sri Lanka's most striking cascades.

Frequently Asked Questions

How tall is Diyaluma Falls?

Diyaluma is one of Sri Lanka's tallest waterfalls, with a dramatic single drop that makes it a striking sight from the base viewpoint.

Is the hike to the infinity pools difficult?

The hike is moderately steep but manageable for most fitness levels, and it rewards visitors with swimmable natural rock pools at the top.

How can I book a Diyaluma Falls Tour?

You can book directly through WhatsApp, the mobile app, or by contacting LetsGo through the website.

Can I swim at Diyaluma Falls?

Yes, the natural infinity pools at the top of the falls are popular for swimming, though caution is advised near the edges.
