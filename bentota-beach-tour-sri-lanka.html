Tour Introduction

About the Destination

Bentota is a well-known west coast resort town, famous for its wide sandy beach, calm waters and the scenic Bentota River.

Why Visit

A top choice for travelers who want a blend of beach relaxation, adventure activities and river excursions in one destination.

Tour Highlights

Enjoy a boat ride to the turtle hatchery, explore the calm river estuary and relax on the wide golden beach.

Activities & Experiences

Try water sports, visit a sea turtle hatchery, take a river safari or explore the nearby Brief Garden.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bentota good for water sports?

Yes, Bentota is one of Sri Lanka's top spots for jet skiing, banana boat rides and other water activities.

Can I visit the turtle hatchery?

Yes, a boat trip along the river can include a stop at a nearby sea turtle hatchery.

Can I combine this with other tours?

Yes, it pairs well with a river safari, Brief Garden visit or other coastal beach tours.

Is transport included?

Absolutely. LetsGo offers transport to and from Bentota and nearby attractions.
