About Safari Tours

Wildlife Experiences

Sri Lanka offers exceptional wildlife encounters with elephants, leopards, monkeys, crocodiles, and a wide variety of birds in their natural habitats.

National Parks

From famous reserves to lesser-known wildlife areas, safari tours bring you close to nature across some of the island's most scenic landscapes.

Why Choose Safari Tours

Travelers choose safari tours for incredible sightings, memorable photography, and the opportunity to experience Sri Lanka's wild side in comfort.

Best Adventure Style

These tours are ideal for couples, families and photographers looking for a unique and exciting trip into the wilderness.

What is included in a safari tour?

Yes, safari tours can be combined with beaches, culture, city sightseeing and custom itineraries.
