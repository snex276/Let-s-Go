Tour Introduction

About Kitulgala

Kitulgala is a small rainforest town on the Kelani River, widely known as Sri Lanka's adventure capital and as the filming location of the Academy Award-winning film The Bridge on the River Kwai.

Why Visit

Travelers come to Kitulgala for thrilling white water rafting, dense rainforest trails, rich birdlife, and a slice of cinematic history along the riverbanks.

Top Places to Visit

Raft the Kelani River's rapids, explore the Makandawa Forest Reserve, and see the concrete foundations that remain from the historic River Kwai bridge set.

Activities & Experiences

Experience white water rafting, canyoning and waterfall abseiling, rainforest hikes, birdwatching, and swimming in calmer stretches of the river.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is white water rafting in Kitulgala safe for beginners?

Yes, most rapids are graded 2-3 and suitable for beginners with an average fitness level, though conditions vary depending on water levels, so always follow the operator's guidance.

How long is the rafting experience?

Most rafting runs cover a stretch of around 5-6.5 km and take approximately 60-90 minutes, depending on the river's flow.

Can I visit the Bridge on the River Kwai film site?

Yes, the concrete foundations from the original film set can still be seen near the river, and many rafting routes pass close by.
